Hi, my name is Grayson Porter Shanks. I am a senior at Case Western Reserve University as Biochemistry B.S. and mathematics minor candidate. I have been involved on campus by being the co-President of Ensemble (French Club), where we create a welcoming environment for those that want to practice or learn French both as a language and or as a culture. I am thankful to be a member of CWRU EMS, which is a collegiate EMS service, for nearly 4 years at this point. I have served as the head driver trainer, and I aim to always encourage others and to educate our new clinical members as they find their way in becoming an EMT. 

Apart from my involvement in clubs, I volunteer with the Center for Civic Engagement and Learning as a student leader. We have been able to help prepare high schoolers for taking their ACT, provide companionship for those in retirement homes, as well as help tutor elementary to middle schoolers in their homework ranging from math to history. 

I also engage in basic science research through the Dr. Levine laboratory. Learn more by accessing the link above. In the lab, I have been exploring the effects of a microbial byproduct, acrolein, and its effect on barrier integrity in the colon. 

When I am not doing homework, or in lab, or something else, I really enjoy my time baking and cooking. My favorite thing to bake is a yule log around Christmas. I also enjoy going to concerts such as Kendrick Lamar, Isaiah Rashad, Zach Bryan, Deftones.

It is my goal to apply to medical school and become a physician. My current plan after I graduate is to move to North Carolina and to work in a hospital for my two gap years until I apply in Spring 2028 and matriculate Fall 2029.
